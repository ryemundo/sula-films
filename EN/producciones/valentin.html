The Killing of the Golden Rooster

SINOPSIS

Following the murder of Valentín Elizalde in Reynosa in 2006, his story became defined by violence, mystery, and the absence of a definitive truth. Through testimonies, archival material, and investigation, the documentary reconstructs the crime and explores three theories surrounding his death: a forbidden romance, ties to organized crime, and betrayal by his inner circle. His death transformed “The Golden Rooster” into a cultural myth that remains alive in the collective memory.
